About the numbers. The label (AC1, AC2, ...) is just the position in this list, so when you delete one the others move up. Each unit keeps a fixed device code underneath, which is what your Pi or ESP32 sends, and a moved-up unit shows it in grey. Deleting an AC permanently removes its readings and its model; live telemetry and past alerts are not affected. Turn off or reconfigure the device first, or its next reading will bring the AC back.
